# Licensing — download, purchase, install, recovery

title: Licensing — download, purchase, key install, trial-to-paid, lost keys
description: How to download the trial, buy a key, install license.json, and recover a lost key

## Downloading the trial

1. Go to **datagatelodge.com** → the Download section
2. Enter your work email (company optional), complete the human check
3. The download starts immediately, and the link is also emailed to you

The Community binary runs the full feature set as a **30-day trial from
first boot** — no license file needed during the trial.

## Buying a perpetual key

- **US$99, one-time**, from the site's Buy button (secure checkout)
- After payment, your license key is emailed to the address used at
  checkout — keep that email; it is your proof of license
- The key removes the 30-day trial limit; everything else you already
  had keeps working

## Installing the key

1. Download `gatelodge-community.exe` if you haven't
2. Save the license block from your email as **`license.json`** in
   Gatelodge's **data folder** — the `data` directory beside the
   executable (create the folder if it doesn't exist)
3. Start (or restart) Gatelodge
4. The console's **License tile** shows your serial and expiry when
   loaded correctly

## Trial → paid behavior

- During the trial: everything works, countdown tracked from first boot
- After the trial without a key: cloud routing stops; **local inference
  keeps working** (embedded models are never time-limited)
- With a key: trial limit removed, perpetual use

## Lost keys and re-sends

If you lose your license email, contact **support@datagatelodge.com**
from the address used at purchase — the key is re-sent after the
purchase record is confirmed. (An automated recovery form is on the
site's support page.)

## Enterprise licensing

Enterprise is a paid binary that will not boot without a license.
Licensing is per user per month, issued per company with named
providers, caps, and expiry presets. Contact sales for terms.
