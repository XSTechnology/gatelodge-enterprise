Checkpoint 02 — The product

The product,
unretouched.

Not mockups. These are our own estate's tools, running in production today — the same software you'd deploy.

◆ How the audit chain verifies

Every request appends a record to a local, append-only ledger — each record carries the hash of the one before it, so any edit anywhere breaks the chain and the console says so. Licenses and key revocations are signed with public-key cryptography and checked against a tamper-evident revocation list. Verification is a command, not a promise.

◆ And the agents that walk through it

The same governance governs what agents do: tool calls pass policy gates, decisions come from versioned rulesets, every run leaves a trace. Ours answer this site's chat, triage our support mail, and decide refund requests live. See agent services →

Checkpoint 09 — Questions at the gate

Asked at the door.
Answered here.

The questions buyers and security teams ask — the Gatekeeper (bottom-right) answers them too, live.

What happens when the 30-day trial ends?

Local inference keeps working — free, forever. Cloud serving pauses until you enter a key: a one-time $99 Community key, or an enterprise license. The check runs locally; nothing phones home.

Does our data pass through your servers?

No. Gatelodge is self-hosted — requests go from your gateway directly to the providers you configured. There is no DataGateLodge SaaS; nothing of yours transits our infrastructure.

How much work is self-hosting, really?

One binary, one command, Windows or Linux. The admin console opens at localhost:8010, provider presets add backends in a click, and your apps point at the endpoint they already speak.

Which models and providers does it support?

Ten backend types — embedded GGUF, llama-server, RunPod, OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, Responses, Azure, Vertex, and Bedrock — all behind one OpenAI-compatible endpoint, swappable per task with alias chains.

What's actually in a license key?

A signed set of claims: edition, expiry, entitled providers, and feature flags. Keys never display secrets, hot-reload without a restart, and are revocable through a tamper-evident list.

Can we try it before talking to anyone?

Yes. The Community binary is the full product on a 30-day trial — take it from the Download page. Or ask the Gatekeeper in the corner: it runs through Gatelodge itself.

How do we get support?

support@datagatelodge.com, the chat widget on this site, or enterprise support with your license. We answer within a business day.
