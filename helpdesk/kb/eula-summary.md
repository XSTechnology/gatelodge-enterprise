## The essentials

- **Licensor**: XS Technologies Inc., a Delaware corporation
  (DataGateLodge is a division). The product is **Gatelodge**.
- **What you get**: a perpetual, non-exclusive, non-transferable
  license to install and run the software **within one organization**
- **Machine code only**: the software is provided as compiled binaries;
  no source code is included or implied
- **No reverse engineering**: decompiling, disassembling, or attempting
  to derive source is prohibited
- **No service-bureau use**: you may not offer Gatelodge to third
  parties as a hosted/managed service on the Community key
- **30-day trial**: the Community trial runs full features for 30 days
  from first boot; local inference remains usable afterward
- **Your data**: the software runs on your infrastructure; prompts and
  logs stay on your systems
- **Warranty & liability**: provided as-is; the agreement caps
  liability (see the full text for the cap and exclusions)
- **Governing law**: Delaware

## Practical answers to common questions

- *Can I run it on multiple machines?* The key licenses one
  organization's internal use — every machine inside that organization
  is covered.
- *Can my customers use my gateway?* Not on a Community key — serving
  third parties requires the Enterprise edition or a channel-partner
  arrangement.
- *Does the license expire?* The perpetual key does not. Enterprise
  licenses carry the term you purchased.
- *Can I get a refund?* Try the free 30-day trial first — it is the
  full product. Refund handling is at the company's discretion;
  contact support.

For anything beyond these points — especially contractual or legal
interpretation — this assistant defers to the support team.
